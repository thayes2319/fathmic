What FATHmic is

FATHmic takes a topic you provide, generates a structured map of the decisions and considerations inside it using AI models, and writes a result based on what you select. It's a starting point for understanding or planning something — not a verified authority. FATHmic isn't a scientist, a doctor, a lawyer, or omniscient. What comes back is only as specific and sound as what you put in and picked. Confirm anything that actually matters against a real, qualified source before acting on it.
